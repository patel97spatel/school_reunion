## How to Use

### Step 1: Go to Preferences
Click "Preferences" in the navigation menu

### Step 2: Select Your Name
Choose from the alphabetically-sorted dropdown list

### Step 3: Choose Your Preference
- 🥗 Click "Vegetarian" for vegetarian
- 🍗 Click "Non-Vegetarian" for non-vegetarian

### Step 4: Submit
Click "Submit Preference"

### Step 5: View Results
- See live counts update instantly
- View full table below
- Use filters to see specific groups

## Filter Options

| Button | Shows |
|--------|-------|
| **All Friends** | All 37 friends regardless of choice |
| **🥗 Vegetarian** | Only friends who chose vegetarian |
| **🍗 Non-Vegetarian** | Only friends who chose non-vegetarian |
| **⏳ Not Selected** | Friends who haven't made a choice yet |

## Live Statistics

Three cards at the top show:
- 🥗 **Vegetarian Count** — How many chose veg
- 🍗 **Non-Vegetarian Count** — How many chose non-veg
- **Total Responses** — Total who have submitted

**Updates automatically after each submission!**
